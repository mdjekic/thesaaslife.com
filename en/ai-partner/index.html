FAQ

Questions you might have

What does “fractional” mean?

Part-time, ongoing involvement. Think of it as having an AI lead on your team for a few hours a week, without a full-time hire. We agree on scope, availability and fee before we start.

Do you actually build things, or just advise?

We build. Workflows, integrations, internal tools and product features — alongside your developers if you have them, or on our own if you don't.

We don't know where to start. Is that a problem?

That's the most common starting point. Tell us your goals and what's slowing you down — finding the right first step is the first part of the work. Sometimes the honest answer is a simpler process, not AI. We'll tell you.

Do we need an in-house technical team?

No. We can build on our own, or work with your existing developers and leave them with everything they need to maintain it.

How much of my team's time will it take?

A few conversations up front with the people doing the work, then short check-ins and testing as we build. We agree on it during the first step.

How does pricing work?

A monthly fee based on scope and involvement, agreed before we start. Get in touch and we'll give you a clear number after our first conversation.

What about our data and security?

Before anything is built, we decide together what systems AI can access, where a human approves, and how customer data is handled. Security is part of the design, not an afterthought.

What happens after the first build?

We review real results, improve what's live and pick the next opportunity. Your team is trained to run what we've built, so you can continue with us — or without us.
